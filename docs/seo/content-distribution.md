# Content Distribution

## Workflow per approved story
1. Publish on primary domain first.
2. Verify story renders and metadata correct.
3. Prepare excerpt + social copy.
4. Distribute to Medium (canonical to primary domain where supported).
5. Share via author networks and relevant communities.
6. Track referrals.

## Canonical strategy
Original on theinkhome.live. Medium syndication uses canonical pointing to primary domain when supported; otherwise excerpt-based distribution.

## Channels
Medium, LinkedIn, writer communities, author networks, RSS. No spam automation.

## Writer relationship
Clear guidelines, transparent review, author profiles, clean URLs, easy sharing.
