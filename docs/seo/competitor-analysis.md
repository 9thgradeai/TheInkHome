# Competitor Analysis (Hypothesis-level)

Relevant competitors: literary publications, online magazines, personal essay outlets, writer communities. No verified data collected here.

Observations (general, not The Ink Home-specific):
- Strong competitors invest in author pages, topic collections, and clear submission flows.
- Many have slow, ad-heavy reading experiences — an opportunity for a fast, calm reader.
- Few offer an integrated local-first drafting tool.

Gaps for The Ink Home:
- Standalone topic/category pages (currently filters).
- Verified author bios/followers (static fallback).
- A small set of high-quality writer resources.

Do not copy branding, content, or design. Differentiate on reading experience and editorial integrity.
